DARK STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy describes how Dark Studio ("the app") handles information obtained from Google accounts. Dark Studio is a personal tool, run locally by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

Data the app accesses

When a Google account is connected, the app requests read-only access through these scopes:

  • youtube.readonly — channel details (name, ID, avatar, subscriber and view counts) and the list of videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — analytics reports for those channels (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources and similar metrics).

If upload features are enabled in the future, the app may also request youtube.upload, used only to publish videos the owner explicitly chooses to upload to their own channels.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display channel information and analytics to the owner inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner's channels.

Storage

OAuth tokens and fetched data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third-party servers other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

Data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Dark Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a channel from the app deletes its stored token and cached data from the local machine.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be opened as an issue at github.com/landherdefi/darkstudio-site.
